About

Inside Atlassian provides our latest announcements, evidence-based insights, and research. Drawing from our own experience, customer case studies, and expert perspectives, we publish practical ideas and clear explanations of how teamwork is changing. By examining how modern organizations connect people, knowledge, and AI, we aim to define what high-performing teamwork looks like now and what it will become next.

Contributors

Experts in their fields and leaders in their own rights, our contributors from both within and outside Atlassian are here to help you make sense of our ever-changing world of work.

Shaina Rozen

Expert in the science of teamwork

Shaina is a writer and content strategist who helps businesses translate complex topics into clear communication their audiences understand. When she’s not wrangling content, she’s busy wrangling her two sons, scoping out local restaurants, and planning her next adventure around the globe.

Genevieve Michaels

Expert in leadership

Genevieve Michaels writes to make complex topics engaging and accessible. She specializes in interviewing experts about how to build happier, healthier, and more innovative workplaces. In addition to Atlassian, she has worked with brands including Adobe, Ramp, Hewlett-Packard Enterprise, and Shopify.

Dr. Molly Sands

Expert in evidence-based practices

Dr. Molly Sands is the head of the Teamwork Lab, which designs evidence-based practices for high-performing teams. She’s passionate about using technology to make it easier for people to do meaningful work together. Prior to Atlassian, Molly held leadership positions at Humu and Chadwick Martin Bailey. Her research has been published in top academic journals and featured in The New York Times, Harvard Business Review, and a TED talk with over six million views.

Kelli María Korducki

Expert in workplace trends

Kelli is an award-winning writer and journalist who contributes to popular publications such as The Atlantic, The Guardian, The New York Times, and Insider. On Inside Atlassian, she dives deep into the latest trends impacting the workplace, offering poignant insights for leaders and individuals to thrive in this era of constant change.

Kat Boogaard

Expert in productivity and teamwork

Kat is a contributing writer focused on the world of work. She’s been published in numerous outlets, including The Muse, Fast Company, and Inc. When she’s not writing, you can find her attempting to convince her two young boys and two rescue dogs to co-exist peacefully.

Sarah Goff Dupont

Inside Atlassian generalist

As one of Atlassian’s longest-tenured employees, Sarah has had a front-row seat to the company’s growth from a small startup to the market behemoth it is today. She shares stories about work-life balance and company culture with this inside-Atlassian knowledge and thoroughly researched insights.
