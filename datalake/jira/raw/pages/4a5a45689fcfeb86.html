The intelligent teamwork platform uniting your system of work

The Atlassian Cloud Platform connects teams with all the curated data, context, and goals they need as they work across their favorite Atlassian and third-party applications.

The Atlassian Platform is the foundation of intelligent connection that empowers Atlassian’s 300K+ customers to do their most impactful work.
