SaaS platforms have boomed in recent years. Now – a happy consequence – SaaS marketplaces are on a similar trajectory of growth. This presents a golden opportunity for developers to solve unique customer challenges by extending the capabilities of platforms through marketplace apps.

Types of SaaS marketplaces: At a glance

With the rapid rise of SaaS marketplaces, the variety of options can be overwhelming. So, where should you start? To help narrow your focus, we’ve outlined the top use cases and their benefits. Understanding where potential customers are most active often reveals the greatest revenue opportunities.

Types of SaaS marketplaces:

  • CRM and help desk: Tools designed to simplify managing customer and prospect interactions. These platforms centralize customer information like purchase history, preferences, and communication records.
  • Workplace and productivity: Products enhance productivity through time tracking, project management, and remote workforce management.
  • Cloud services and infrastructure: Solutions covering servers, storage, networking, and payment systems.
  • E-commerce: Platforms enabling online retail, with features like product cataloging, payment integration, and order tracking.
  • Communication and collaboration: Tools that support real-time communication and teamwork, including file sharing, instant messaging, calendars, and task management.

With this understanding, let’s dive into the ultimate list of SaaS marketplaces for developers.

Checklist: How developers choose the best SaaS marketplace

With so many SaaS marketplaces for developers to choose from, how do you decide which to develop with?

To help you to figure this out, here are some things you should consider when narrowing down your options:

  • User base: The ideal marketplace provides 3rd-party developers with opportunities to address a wide range of industries and use cases. So looks at how many customers the SaaS platform already has. And ask key questions such as do they readily use the app? And are they in your target demographic?
  • Developer support: Look for SaaS marketplaces that provide support for developers – like resources and documentation.
  • Integration: Make sure the app provides robust and readily available API access, with strong documentation. Plus, check out the types of building blocks (or components) the platform allows you, as a developer, to interact with – this can help identify the scope of the solutions you can create.
  • Reviews: Platforms with strong and positive customer feedback are your best bet because they’ll likely stand the test of time, and capture a greater market share.
  • Competition: Consider how crowded the current marketplace is. If the marketplace is already saturated with apps, it makes competition harder. On the other hand, less crowded marketplaces may offer you the opportunity to be the first one to offer various solutions and enjoy the prime.

In light of all this, it’s worth considering which marketplaces particularly stand out as potential opportunities. Make a short list of your top platforms and their user base, and see which best aligns with your target audience.
